# 📊 Telco Customer Churn Analysis
## Executive Summary

### 🎯 Project Objective
The goal of this project is to analyze customer data and identify factors influencing customer churn. Understanding these patterns helps businesses improve customer retention and increase revenue.

---

## 🔍 Key Insights

### 👥 Customer Demographics
- Total Customers: **7,043**
- Male Customers: **50.5%**
- Female Customers: **49.5%**
- Senior Citizens: **16.2%**

### 📄 Contract Analysis
- Month-to-Month contracts dominate the customer base.
- Customers with long-term contracts show better retention.

### 🌐 Internet Service Distribution
- Fiber Optic: **44%**
- DSL: **34%**
- No Internet Service: **22%**

### 📈 Customer Retention Trends
- Customers with month-to-month contracts are more likely to churn.
- Long-term customers demonstrate stronger loyalty.
- Service quality and customer support play a major role in retention.

---

## 💡 Business Recommendations

### 1️⃣ Increase Long-Term Contract Adoption
Offer discounts and rewards for annual and two-year contracts.

### 2️⃣ Customer Loyalty Program
Provide cashback, bonus data, and exclusive benefits for loyal customers.

### 3️⃣ Improve Fiber Optic Experience
Focus on network quality and issue resolution to improve customer satisfaction.

### 4️⃣ Personalized Retention Campaigns
Target high-risk customers with special offers before they leave.

### 5️⃣ Senior Citizen Benefits
Introduce affordable plans and dedicated support services.

| Initiative | Expected Outcome |

## ✅ Conclusion

The Telco Customer Churn Dashboard provides valuable insights into customer behavior, contract preferences, and service usage patterns. Implementing the recommended strategies can help reduce churn, improve customer satisfaction, and drive long-term business growth.
