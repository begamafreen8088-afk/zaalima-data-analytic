# Week 1 Day 3 - Contract, Tenure and Churn Analysis

## Objective
Analyze relationship between contract type, customer tenure and churn.

## Analysis Performed


## Contract Distribution

- Month-to-month: 3,875 customers
- One year: 1,473 customers
- Two year: 1,695 customers

Most customers are on month-to-month contracts.

## Churn Distribution

- No churn: 5,174 customers
- Yes churn: 1,869 customers

Around 26.5% of customers have churned.

## Average Tenure by Churn

- Customers who did not churn: 37.57 months
- Customers who churned: 17.98 months

This indicates that customers with shorter tenure are more likely to churn.

## Contract vs Churn

| Contract | No Churn | Churn |
|----------|----------|--------|
| Month-to-month | 2220 | 1655 |
| One year | 1307 | 166 |
| Two year | 1647 | 48 |

## Key Finding

Month-to-month customers have a much higher churn rate compared with customers on one-year or two-year contracts.

Longer-term contracts appear to be associated with significantly lower churn.

## Conclusion

- Month-to-month customers have highest churn.
- Customers with longer contracts churn less.
- Customers who churn have lower average tenure.
