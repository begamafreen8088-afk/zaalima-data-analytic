# 🚀 Telco Customer Churn Analysis
## Final Executive Report

### 📌 Business Problem

Customer churn directly impacts revenue, customer acquisition costs, and long-term business growth. This project aims to identify customer behavior patterns and uncover factors contributing to customer attrition.

---

# 🎯 Key Findings

## 🔴 Churn Risk Concentrated in Month-to-Month Customers
Customers using month-to-month contracts show significantly higher churn risk compared to customers with one-year or two-year contracts.

### Business Impact
Higher customer turnover increases acquisition costs and reduces profitability.

---

## 🌐 Internet Service Influences Customer Retention
Fiber Optic and DSL customers represent the largest customer segments.

### Business Impact
Service quality improvements can significantly improve customer satisfaction and retention.

---

## 👥 Customer Demographics
Customer distribution is balanced across gender categories, indicating that churn is influenced more by service and contract factors than demographics.

### Business Impact
Retention strategies should focus on customer experience rather than demographic targeting.

---

## 📈 Customer Loyalty and Tenure
Long-tenure customers demonstrate stronger loyalty and lower churn probability.

### Business Impact
Retaining existing customers is more cost-effective than acquiring new customers.

---

# 💡 Strategic Recommendations

### 1. Contract Conversion Strategy
Encourage customers to migrate from month-to-month contracts to annual plans through discounts and loyalty rewards.

### 2. Predictive Retention Campaigns
Identify high-risk customers and proactively provide personalized offers before churn occurs.

### 3. Service Excellence Program
Improve network reliability and customer support response times.

### 4. Customer Loyalty Initiative
Reward long-term customers with exclusive benefits, discounts, and premium support.

### 5. Data-Driven Decision Making
Use dashboards regularly to monitor customer trends and support strategic business decisions.
